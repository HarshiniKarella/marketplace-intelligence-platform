# Business Requirements Document

## Project Name

MarketPulse – Marketplace Operations Intelligence Platform

---

## Business Problem

Modern e-commerce marketplaces generate data from customers, sellers, products, payments, deliveries, and reviews.

Although this data exists, it is often distributed across multiple operational tables, making it difficult for business teams to monitor marketplace performance and make timely decisions.

This project aims to centralize marketplace data into a production-style analytics platform that supports business intelligence, operational monitoring, and predictive analytics.

---

## Intended Users

- Operations Managers
- Seller Success Team
- Customer Experience Team
- Business Analysts
- Data Scientists

---

## Business Goals

- Monitor marketplace performance
- Track seller performance
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Monitor delivery performance
- Analyze product categories
- Predict orders at risk of delayed delivery
- Support data-driven operational decisions

---

## Success Metrics

- Marketplace KPIs are available through dashboards.
- Historical data is stored in PostgreSQL.
- Automated ETL pipelines populate analytical tables.
- Machine learning predicts delivery delay risk.
- Predictions are accessible through REST APIs.
- Business users can explore results through interactive dashboards.
